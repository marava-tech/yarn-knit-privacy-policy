Privacy Policy — Yarn Knit: Color Sort Puzzle

Application: Yarn Knit: Color Sort Puzzle (package tech.marava.yarnknit)
Developer: Marava • Last updated: September 17, 2026

The Summary: You do not need an account to play. The core game works 100% offline without requiring internet access. We never sell your personal data. When online, the app connects to Google services for crash reporting and analytics to improve game performance.

1. Information We Collect

A. Core Gameplay & Local Device Storage

Your puzzle progress, stars, unlocked levels, sound settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard Android secure preferences. You can play without an internet connection.

B. Crash Diagnostics & Stability (Firebase Crashlytics)

To ensure high performance and fix bugs quickly, we use Firebase Crashlytics (by Google). If the app experiences an unexpected error or crash, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information is collected:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • System state and available memory at the moment of the crash
  • Stack traces and error codes

C. Performance & Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to analyze general aggregate engagement to make levels fair and enjoyable:

  • Game session start/stop and level completion events
  • Anonymous app instance identifier (resettable by reinstalling or clearing app storage)
  • General approximate geographical region (country level derived from IP)

D. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and may display non-intrusive ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and interaction metrics per Google's advertising policies. You may reset or opt-out of personalized ads in your Android Settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

2. In-App Purchases

Any in-app purchases (such as ad removal) are processed securely through the official Google Play Billing service. We never collect, see, or store your credit card or financial payment details.

3. Children's Privacy

Yarn Knit: Color Sort Puzzle is a general-audience casual puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete such information.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because the game does not require account creation, uninstalling the app or clearing the app storage in your Android settings deletes all locally stored game data immediately. For any inquiries regarding analytics data deletion, please contact us at our email address below.
